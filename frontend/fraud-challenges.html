Fraud Detection Challenge

Level 1: Email Phishing

Examine the following scenario and determine if it's legitimate or a scam.

SUSPICIOUS
Bank Account Verification Required
From: security-alert@bank-verification.com
Subject: URGENT: Your Account Will Be Suspended
Dear Valued Customer,

We have detected suspicious activity on your account. Your account will be suspended within 24 hours unless you verify your information immediately.

Click here to verify your account now:
www.bank-secure-login.verify.com/urgent-verification

You will need to provide:
• Your username and password
• Credit card details
• Social security number

Act now to avoid account closure!

Bank Security Team
Is this email legitimate or a phishing scam?

Red Flags Identified:

  • Suspicious sender domain (not official bank domain)
  • Creates false urgency ("suspended within 24 hours")
  • Requests sensitive information (passwords, SSN)
  • Suspicious URL that doesn't match bank's official website
  • Generic greeting ("Dear Valued Customer")
  • Threatening language about account closure
LEGITIMATE
SMS from Bank
From: HDFC Bank (HD-HDFCBK)
Message: SMS Alert
Dear Customer,

Your HDFC Bank account ending in 1234 has been credited with Rs. 50,000 on 21-Jan-2025 at 14:30.

Available balance: Rs. 1,25,000

If you did not initiate this transaction, please contact customer care at 1800-425-4332 immediately.

- HDFC Bank
Is this SMS from your bank legitimate or suspicious?

Why This is Legitimate:

  • Official bank sender ID (HD-HDFCBK)
  • Specific account details (last 4 digits)
  • Clear transaction information with timestamp
  • Provides official customer care number
  • No links or requests for personal information
  • Professional tone and proper bank branding
SCAM
Phone Call Scam
Caller: "Bank Customer Service"
Caller ID: +91-XXXXXXXXXX (Unknown number)
"Hello, this is calling from your bank's fraud prevention department. We've detected unauthorized transactions on your account worth Rs. 75,000.

To secure your account, I need to verify some information:
• What's your ATM PIN?
• Can you tell me the CVV on your debit card?
• What's your internet banking password?

Sir, this is very urgent. Your money will be lost if you don't act now!

I can also help you transfer your money to a 'safe account' temporarily while we investigate."
How should you respond to this phone call?

Major Red Flags:

  • Bank NEVER asks for PIN, CVV, or passwords over phone
  • Creates panic with "urgent" and "money will be lost"
  • Asks you to transfer money to "safe account"
  • Unknown caller ID (banks use official numbers)
  • Requests immediate action without proper verification
  • No legitimate bank offers "safe account" transfers
SOPHISTICATED SCAM
Investment App Fraud
App: "CryptoGain Pro" (Downloaded from Google Play)
Message: In-app notification
🎉 CONGRATULATIONS! 🎉

Your AI trading bot has generated 340% returns in just 7 days!
Current Balance: ₹4,68,000 (Initial: ₹1,00,000)

🏆 TOP TRADER BONUS: Claim ₹50,000 extra!

To withdraw your profits, complete KYC verification:
• Upload Aadhaar & PAN
• Pay ₹5,000 processing fee (refundable)
• Provide bank statement for tax compliance

⚡ Limited time: Complete within 2 hours or lose your profits!

"I made ₹2 lakhs in one week!" - Verified User Review ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
This investment app is showing huge returns and asking for KYC. What should you do?

Advanced Scam Red Flags:

  • Unrealistic returns (340% in 7 days is impossible)
  • Asks for upfront fees to withdraw "profits"
  • Creates artificial urgency (2-hour deadline)
  • Fake user reviews and testimonials
  • Requests sensitive KYC documents before verification
  • Processing fees for withdrawals (legitimate platforms don't charge)
  • Uses gambling psychology with "bonuses" and "limited time"
DEEPFAKE SCAM
AI-Generated Video Call
Video Call: WhatsApp Video Call
Caller: Your "Boss" (CEO of your company)
Display Name: Matches your actual boss's name
📹 Video shows your boss speaking:

"Hi [Your Name], I'm in an urgent client meeting in Dubai. There's been a last-minute compliance issue with our biggest client.

I need you to immediately transfer ₹2,50,000 from the company account to this vendor account for emergency documentation fees:
Account: 1234567890, IFSC: SBIN0001234

This is extremely confidential - don't discuss with anyone, not even the finance team. The client will cancel the ₹50 crore deal if we don't act now.

I'll reimburse and explain everything tomorrow. Just send me the transaction screenshot on my personal WhatsApp: +971-XXXXXXXX

The video quality is decent, voice matches your boss, and they know internal company details."
Your "boss" is asking you to transfer company money urgently via video call. What do you do?

Deepfake Scam Indicators:

  • Requests money transfer outside normal approval process
  • Asks for secrecy ("don't discuss with anyone")
  • Provides different WhatsApp number (not known official number)
  • Creates extreme urgency to bypass verification
  • Video call quality may be slightly off or delayed
  • Uses fear tactics ("₹50 crore deal will be cancelled")
  • Asks for screenshot evidence to scammer's personal number
  • Always verify independently through known official channels
LEGITIMATE
Genuine Security Alert
From: security@yourbank.com
Subject: New Device Login Alert
Bank: State Bank of India (Official domain)
Dear Mr. Sharma,

We noticed a login to your SBI account from a new device:
Device: Samsung Galaxy S23 (Android 13)
Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra
Time: 15-Jan-2025, 2:30 PM IST
IP Address: 103.XXX.XXX.XXX

If this was you, no action is needed. Your account remains secure.

If this wasn't you:
• Login to your account through official SBI website
• Change your password immediately
• Call customer care: 1800 11 2211

This is an automated security notification. Please do not reply to this email.

Best regards,
SBI Digital Security Team

This email was sent to raj.sharma@gmail.com. SBI will never ask for passwords via email.
You receive this login notification from your bank. How should you evaluate this?

Why This is Legitimate:

  • Sent from official bank domain (@yourbank.com)
  • Specific device and location information
  • Doesn't ask for any sensitive information
  • Provides clear next steps without urgent language
  • Includes official customer care number
  • Explicitly states "do not reply" for automated alerts
  • Professional tone and proper bank formatting
  • Disclaimer about not asking for passwords
ADVANCED SOCIAL ENGINEERING
Romance + Investment Scam
Platform: Instagram DM
Profile: @sophia_trader_dubai (10k followers, verified-looking)
Conversation: 3 weeks of daily chatting
💕 "Hi love! I've been thinking about our future together...

You know how I mentioned my forex trading success? I just made $15,000 profit this week! 📈

I want to help you become financially independent too. My mentor (a Wall Street expert) is offering a special training program for just 5 people this month.

Normal cost: $5,000
For you (because I care): Just $2,000 💝

You'll learn my exact strategy that made me $200k last year. I can even lend you trading capital once you complete the course!

My other students are already making $500-1000 daily. Sarah from Mumbai made $3,000 in her first week! 🚀

Only 2 spots left... should I reserve one for you? The program starts Monday!

Trust me baby, this will change our lives together ❤️"
After 3 weeks of romantic conversations, this person is now offering investment training. What's happening?

Romance + Investment Scam Signs:

  • Builds emotional connection first, then asks for money
  • Claims exclusive access to profitable trading secrets
  • Uses fake success stories and testimonials
  • Creates artificial scarcity ("only 2 spots left")
  • Offers to lend money after you pay for training
  • Mixes romance language with financial promises
  • Pushes for quick decision without due diligence
  • Profile likely uses stolen photos of attractive person
MASTER-LEVEL SCAM
Sophisticated Banking Trojan
Email: security-update@hdfcbank.com
Subject: Critical Security Update Required
Received: Looks identical to real HDFC emails
Dear Valued Customer,

As part of our ongoing security enhancements, HDFC Bank is implementing advanced encryption protocols to protect your account.

What you need to do:
1. Download the mandatory security update
2. Install "HDFC Security Suite" on your device
3. Complete one-time verification process

🔒 Download Link: hdfc-security-update.apk
📱 Compatible with all Android devices
⚡ Installation takes 2 minutes
Why this is mandatory:
• RBI compliance requirement (effective 15-Jan-2025)
• Prevents unauthorized access to your account
• Required for continued mobile banking access

Accounts without security update will be temporarily restricted from 20-Jan-2025.

For technical support: Call 1800-202-6161
(This matches real HDFC customer care number)

Best regards,
HDFC Bank Cyber Security Team

HDFC Bank Ltd. Regd. Office: HDFC Bank House, Senapati Bapat Marg, Lower Parel, Mumbai 400013
This email looks very official and matches HDFC's formatting perfectly. The phone number is correct. What should you do?

Master-Level Scam Detection:

  • Banks NEVER send security software via email attachments
  • Legitimate bank apps are only available on official app stores
  • APK files from emails are almost always malware
  • Uses real customer care number to appear legitimate
  • Mimics official bank design and language perfectly
  • Creates compliance urgency (RBI requirement)
  • Threatens account restriction to force quick action
  • Always verify security updates through official bank channels
